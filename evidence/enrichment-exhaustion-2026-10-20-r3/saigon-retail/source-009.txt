Complete service | TAG Heuer BACK TO ALL SERVICES COMPLETE SERVICE Your mechanical watch contains more than a hundred pieces which work synchronously 24/7. With time, the constant friction can wear out the pieces and oils dry. A full service restores both the functional and aesthetic appearance of your watch. It includes disassembly, overhaul, replacement of worn components as well as ultrasonic cleaning of the case whose sealing will be restored. Maintenance recommended every 5 years All movements parts included Warranty coverage for 24 months STEP 1: COMPLETE WATCH DISMANTLING The watch is opened and its movement is carefully removed from the case. Both of them will be fully dismantled in spare parts to be ready for cleaning. STEP 2: CLEANING All spare parts will be placed one by one in watchmaking cleaning baskets specially designed for ultrasonic baths. Depending on the sensitivity and material of a part, various cleaning solutions and devices can be used to remove all grease and dirt residues. STEP 3: COMPONENTS REPLACEMENT All components are being examined by TAG Heuer's watchmaker. Worn components are being replaced by TAG Heuer original components. STEP 4: MOVEMENT ADJUSTMENT Movement is assembled and specific points are being lubricated to ensure movement long lasting. Accuracy is checked to guarantee that movement performances are respecting TAG Heuer high requirements. STEP 5: CASE ASSEMBLY Movement, dial, hands and case are being re-assembled. All seals are replaced to ensure watch water-resistancy. STEP 6: QUALITY CONTROL AND WARRANTY Watch functions, aesthetic and performance are being thoroughly controled. Power reserve is checked against TAG Heuer specification to ensure highest accuracy. Your watch is covered by a 2 years warranty.
