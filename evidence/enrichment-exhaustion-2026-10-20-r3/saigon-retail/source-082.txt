For the Ben Thanh and Bui Thi Xuan area, the bank offices below provide current, identifiable destinations. Around Dong Khoi and Nguyen Hue, Vietcombank also lists appointed hotel exchange agents. An office’s existence does not mean it accepts every foreign currency, damaged note or amount without conditions. Tell the bank which currency and denominations you have and ask whether it can buy those notes for VND. Take your passport for identification and ask for the receipt and full calculation. “All currencies” and “unlimited cash” are not useful promises for a traveller. A bank can quote both cash and transfer rates while having different availability at different offices. If your currency is not commonly handled, confirmation before the journey matters more than the number of bank names in a list.
