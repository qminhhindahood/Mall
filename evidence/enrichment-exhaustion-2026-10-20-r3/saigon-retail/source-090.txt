L122: How should I clean my shoes?
L123: 
L124: Clean your running shoes and other athletic shoes with a mild, dye-free detergent (either for dishwashing or laundry) and a bristle brush (such as a toothbrush) following the steps below.
L125:   1. Test a small amount of the detergent on an unnoticeable part of your shoes to make sure it won’t stain them.
L126:   2. Use your bristle brush to scrub the shoes with the detergent.
L127:   3. Wipe shoes clean with a damp cloth.
L128:   4. Allow your shoes to air dry for 24 hours before wearing.
L129: 
L130: For shoes made of suede or leather, use a specialty leather or suede cleaner.
L131: We do not suggest putting any of our shoes into the washing machine or dryer since many of our materials should not be fully submerged in water or exposed to heat.
