Boat Noodle Thai Restaurant in District 1 | PinGuide Home / Places / Boat Noodle Boat Noodle Tầng B3, Vincom Center Đồng Khởi, 72 Lê Thánh Tôn, Phường Bến Nghé, Quận 1, Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh, Vietnam Open in Google Maps ↗ 5 min walk from Opera House metro station 10:00-22:00 daily ☎️ 02838256625 Boat Noodle is a Thai restaurant in the Vincom Center Đồng Khởi on Lê Thánh Tôn street, District 1, Ho Chi Minh City. It occupies a space on the B3 basement level of the shopping mall. The restaurant specializes in Thai street food dishes. Its menu centers on its namesake dish, kuay teow reua, or Thai boat noodles. The kitchen prepares small bowls of noodles in rich, spiced broths. It serves classic noodle styles like Ayutthaya and Pathumthani. The menu also features other Thai standards. These include Tom Yum soup, grilled pork skewers known as mooping, and various gỏi salads. Toppings and side dishes are available as additions to the main noodle bowls. The restaurant provides a casual dining environment within the commercial center. The interior design reflects elements of Bangkok’s vibrant street culture. The setting is modern and suitable for mall shoppers. The restaurant serves families, individuals, and small groups. It offers a fixed menu of Thai food and drinks. The beverage list includes Thai milk tea and other traditional refreshments. The restaurant is part of a larger chain with multiple locations in the city. The original boat noodle dishes were sold from boats in Thailand’s canals. This restaurant brings that street-food concept into a shopping mall.
