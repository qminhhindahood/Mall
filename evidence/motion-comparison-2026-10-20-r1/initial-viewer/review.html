Why motion can be missing on the live website

  • The live site remembers the introduction’s Pause choice. Use its Resume button to restore automatic rotation.
  • A browser reduced-motion request turns off the automatic presentation and reduces spatial transitions. This viewer leaves that preference unchanged; recordings play only after an explicit Play or Replay action.
  • A direct floor, store or Discovery link starts later in the journey. The fresh introduction links below use view=exterior.
  • A restored scroll position can also place you past the introduction. The recordings always start from their own first frame.
