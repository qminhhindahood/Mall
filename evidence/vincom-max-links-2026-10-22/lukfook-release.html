Grand Opening of Lukfook Jewellery’s New Shop in Vincom Dong Khoi Shopping Centre Jointly Celebrating Brand's 35th Anniversary

(7 September 2026) — Luk Fook Holdings (International) Limited (“Lukfook” or the “Group”) (Stock Code: 0590) is pleased to announce the grand opening of Lukfook Jewellery’s Shop at Vincom Center Dong Khoi, Ho Chi Minh City, on 1 September. The grand opening ceremony featured guests of honour including Ms. Huyen Baby, Famous Artist, Ms. Pham Thi Thu Hien, General Director of Vincom Center Dong Khoi, Mr. Tan Timothy, Vice President of Sales & Marketing Development of InterContinental Grand Ho Tram. They were accompanied by Ms. Wong Hau Yeung, Shirley, Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer of Lukfook Group, Mr. Lin Chu Xiong, Mr. Lin Jia Tian, Ms. Nguyễn Hà, Mr. Wu Ming Song and Ms. He Xuan, Directors of Lukfook Jewellery Vietnam Shop, to unveil the new shop with all distinguished guests.

Mr. Wong Wai Sheung, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Lukfook Group, said, “Lukfook has always been actively seeking overseas business opportunities. Vietnam, as one of the most dynamic markets in Southeast Asia, has also become a key market for Lukfook to expand its overseas footprint. Coupled with the Group celebrating its milestone 35th anniversary on September 1, the opening of our Vincom Center Dong Khoi shop marks a delightful double celebrations. Looking ahead, the Group will continue to deepen its footprint in Vietnam, expanding into high-potential cities like Da Nang and Nha Trang to build a comprehensive retail network, capitalise on market opportunities, and further realise its corporate vision of 'Brand of Hong Kong, Sparkling the World’.”

The Group firmly believes that by pairing Ho Chi Minh City’s vibrant international appeal with Vincom Center Dong Khoi's prime location and heavy foot traffic, this partnership will yield strong synergies and showcase Lukfook Jewellery’s exceptional craftsmanship and brand prestige to a broader Vietnamese consumer base.

Address: Unit B1-17AB, Vincom Center Dong Khoi, No.72 Le Thanh Ton, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam
