Label Lab

Label Lab is a high-end contemporary and high-street multi-brand complex located in Saigon Center, bridging international streetwear and the 'Hypebeast' community in Vietnam. Label Lab boasts a curated and continuously updated catalog from influential names on the global streetwear map such as Off-White, Palm Angels, MISBHV, and Evisu.
